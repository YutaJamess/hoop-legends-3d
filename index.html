HOOP LEGENDS 3D

HALF COURT 1 ON 1 · FIRST TO 21 OR 3:00

Both players attack the same basket. WASD move, SHIFT sprint. Hold SPACE to shoot and release in the green zone. On defense SPACE jumps to block and E steals. With the ball, E is a crossover. After a steal or rebound you must take the ball back behind the arc before you can shoot. Beyond the arc counts three. Press F near the rim to dunk; it becomes a contact dunk if a defender is in the way. FREE SHOOT removes the defender and feeds the ball back. ESC returns here.
